A graph-first view

Context changes the picture.

AML Graph treats transfers as a temporal, directed network—so a suspicious pattern can be read as connected behavior, not isolated events.

The model

Every edge keeps its place in time.

Transactions remain distinct, including parallel transfers. The graph orders each edge by timestamp and transaction ID, then detector rules search for time-respecting structures.

ABCDE
01 / DETECT

Search for known structures.

Windowed fan patterns, gather and scatter motifs, time-respecting cycles, and rapid pass-through paths are found with deterministic rules and train-derived settings.

02 / CONNECT

Bring related evidence together.

Hits can merge when accounts and time spans connect, while high-degree hubs are excluded as merge links. Each resulting cluster retains its accounts, transactions, and contributing hits.

03 / EXPLAIN

Keep the evidence visible.

Ranked alerts carry transaction paths and model features. Deterministic summaries remain available as the narrative fallback.
